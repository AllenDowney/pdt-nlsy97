# pdt-nlsy97

Metadata for the [National Longitudinal Survey of Youth 1997 (NLSY97)](https://www.nlsinfo.org/content/cohorts/nlsy97), built to the [PublicDataToolkit](https://github.com/AllenDowney/PublicDataToolkit) standard.

**Status:** started from the PublicDataToolkit template; no records yet.

## What the dataset is

BLS's panel survey of about 9,000 people born in 1980-84, interviewed annually from 1997 to 2011 and every two years since; managed by CHRR at The Ohio State University, interviews by NORC.

## Terms of use

The terms are at https://www.nlsinfo.org/system-use-notification. They haven't been reviewed yet, so nothing built from this repo may ship (STANDARD.md §8). Once a person has decided them, summarize them here; the decision is in [`dataset.yaml`](dataset.yaml).

## How to get the data

The files are listed in [`sources.yaml`](sources.yaml), with where to get each and its checksum.

## How to use the metadata

- `metadata/releases/<release>/variables.jsonl`: one record per variable, with where each fact came from.
- `docs/`: a page per variable, generated by `make views`.
- `agent/`: shards for LLMs; start with `agent/SKILL.md`.

## How to cite

Bureau of Labor Statistics, U.S. Department of Labor. National Longitudinal Survey of Youth 1997 cohort, 1997-2023 (rounds 1-21). Produced and distributed by the Center for Human Resource Research (CHRR), The Ohio State University. Columbus, OH.

## How to rebuild

With the sources in `data/raw/` and a PublicDataToolkit checkout next to this repo:

```bash
make build
make views
make check
```
